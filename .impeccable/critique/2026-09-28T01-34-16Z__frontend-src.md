## Design Specificity Verdict

Low. Without the fingerprints the shell is the stock shadcn neutral preset: icon-tile
logo, an empty 48px header holding only the sidebar toggle, icon tiles on panel headers
(including a Sparkles tile on Findings, an AI/SaaS tell). The dual-strand fingerprint
is the only product-specific element, and on Compare it is framed like a settings card.
Deterministic scan: `impeccable detect --json frontend/src` exited 0 with no findings.

## What's Working

- The fingerprint grammar is honest and memorable: energy above the axis, brightness
  below, beats on top, shared scale, documented constants.
- The A/B selection model: outline + badge + chip, `aria-pressed`, no nested buttons.
- Selecting a card and seeing its mini fingerprint take the slot colour.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Compare layout hides the comparison.** Upload dropzones sit between the two
  charts and above loaded tracks; the filled "Play" button outweighs the data. Fix: one
  shared time axis with A directly above B, upload collapsed to a secondary "Replace
  file" action, findings next to the evidence.
- **[P1] Violet carries two meanings (slot A and energy).** Decision taken for now:
  keep violet/cyan as data colours and identify slots with explicit A/B markers; no
  system-wide colour change yet. On Compare, put the SlotBadge and track title in each
  panel header.
- **[P2] Fingerprint has no time axis or visible scale.** Add a time ruler and a scale
  caption; hide or distinguish the playhead at t=0; consider click-to-seek.
- **[P2] Generic shell and component chrome.** Shell part addressed by the
  shell-coherence change (shared surface, no desktop header, plain wordmark). Remaining:
  Music/Sparkles icon tiles on SongPanel and Findings, nested border in Fingerprint,
  loud genre filter buttons.
- **[P3] Radius steps are mixed.** A shared radius scale exists (`--radius` with derived
  `--radius-sm` to `--radius-4xl` tokens), but components draw from many steps of it:
  cards `rounded-xl`, dropzone `rounded-lg`, fingerprint box and badges `rounded-md`,
  legend swatches `rounded-sm`, play buttons `rounded-full`, plus pill-shaped bar ends
  (`rx = barWidth / 2`). Fix: settle on one or two steps of the existing scale.
  Bar-end changes are deferred to a dedicated fingerprint/Compare pass.

## Persona Red Flags

- **First-time visitor:** no explanation of energy/brightness or per-track preview
  scaling; `/compare` with no selection shows two blank upload boxes and no contextual route back to the Library (only the
  sidebar navigation).
- **Power user:** no swap, no "keep A, change B", no sort, no seek; findings show no numbers.
- **Phone user:** titles truncate, the sticky selection bar covers the last card's
  metadata; (fixed by shell change) no product name in the phone header.

## Minor Observations

- "0 of 2 selected" bar always visible with disabled buttons.
- Skeleton count 8 for a 5-track library.
- MiniFingerprint `preserveAspectRatio="none"` stretches bars with card width.
- Beat dots sit at y=4, detached from the bars.
- Filename on Compare uses `font-mono`, which falls back to a system mono (no Geist Mono installed).
- Findings would scan better with Song A / Song B marked.

## Deferred design work (not scheduled)

Recorded as future work; the owner asked that none of these be implemented in the
shell-coherence or Home branches:

1. Compare: shared time axis, A above B, upload as secondary "Replace file".
2. Compare: SlotBadge + track title in panel headers; remove Music/Sparkles icon tiles.
3. Fingerprint: time ruler, scale caption, playhead-at-zero treatment, click-to-seek,
   remove the nested bordered box, bar-end shape (dedicated pass).
4. Compare: A/B swap, keep-A-change-B flow, clearer errors with retry, align `accept`
   with the "MP3 or WAV" copy.
5. Library: quieter genre filter, selection bar hidden or reworded until the first pick,
   skeleton count matching the library, phone title truncation and bar overlap.
6. Consistent use of the existing radius scale across Library/Compare components.
7. Explanations for energy, brightness, shared scale and per-track previews (partly the
   job of the planned Home page).
8. Shell, found while verifying the shell-coherence change. Both predate it:
   choosing a nav link inside the phone slide-out menu does not close the menu; and when
   the desktop sidebar is collapsed its off-screen navigation links stay in the Tab order
   (shadcn offcanvas behaviour). The collapse toggle's placement in the sidebar header
   was introduced by the shell-coherence change, which also handles its focus: it leaves
   the Tab order while collapsed, and focus moves to the visible toggle on collapse and
   reopen.
